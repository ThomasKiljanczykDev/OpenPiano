OpenPiano turns your phone or tablet into a real touchscreen piano, with low-latency sound and true multitouch — play chords, hold notes, and slide between keys just like on a physical keyboard.
No ads, ever — nothing interrupts your playing.

Features:

• No ads, no in-app purchases, no account needed.
• Low-latency audio engine, tuned for the shortest possible delay between touch and sound.
• Full multitouch: play as many notes at once as your fingers allow, with press-and-hold sustain and glissando.
• Configurable keyboard width, from 7 to 21 visible white keys, with an overview strip showing your position across the full 88-key range.
• Octave and single-key shift controls to reach the full range of the piano.
• Key label modes: off, C-only, or every note name.
• USB MIDI output — plug your device into a computer or audio interface and play a DAW, synth, or any other MIDI-capable gear with the app's keyboard.
• A warm acoustic grand piano sound, sampled from a real Yamaha C5 (Salamander Grand Piano, CC BY 3.0), plus reverb and a limiter.
• Available in English, German, Spanish, French, Polish, and Brazilian Portuguese.

OpenPiano is free and open-source software, released under the GNU General Public License v3 or later.
The source code is available on GitHub: https://github.com/ThomasKiljanczykDev/OpenPiano

This build uses Firebase Analytics (automatic collection only — active users, country, city; no custom events, no advertising ID) and Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports, disabled in debug builds.
A build with neither, for F-Droid and direct download, is available from the project's GitHub releases.
